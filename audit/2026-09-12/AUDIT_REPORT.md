# BURGAMOTS Adversarial Audit Report

## 1. Scope and verdict

### Audited artifact

- `AlkaiDynamics/BURGAMOTS`
- branch `main`
- commit `8a9029b69b107d4230c9f053b4c8ef545a99e90e`
- audit date 2026-09-12

### Governing question

Does the audited BURGAMOTS artifact fulfill its **original owner-supplied purpose as a falsification/validation instrument for zodiacal/astronomical relational efficacy**?

### Overall verdict

**Does not currently establish a valid falsification test.**

The repository is a substantive interactive survey/visualization prototype with a simplified orbital visualizer and a detailed public heliophysics narrative. However, the original zodiac/relational falsification target is not operationalized in the artifact. The null/control, unit of analysis, sampling frame, evaluation boundary, decisive input provenance, and claim→run→data chain required by the audit contract are absent or unresolved.

Separately, the repository documentation states that a complete DeepXDE/PINN scientific engine, preprocessed JPL data, notebooks, and custom losses are available in the repository, but those artifacts are not present at the frozen commit. The visible “validation” quantities are encoded as source literals or mock arrays rather than generated by an inspectable scientific pipeline. Therefore the artifact cannot establish its displayed hindcast, forecast, correlation, significance, blind-test, uncertainty, or causal-mechanism claims.

This verdict **does not establish that the underlying hypotheses are false** and does not establish that external/private experiments never existed. It establishes that the audited repository cannot support those claims or its original falsification purpose from the evidence available at the cutoff.

### What was not audited

- No product code was changed.
- No successor design was created.
- No independent new astrology/heliophysics experiment was performed.
- External/private artifacts not linked and inspectable from the audited record were not presumed to exist or not exist.
- Literature citations were not exhaustively re-litigated; the audit asks whether project-specific conclusions are traceable.

## 4. Adversarial interpretations

### Strongest defense of BURGAMOTS

The repository can reasonably be interpreted as a **concept/survey frontend** rather than the scientific compute repository. Hardcoded charts, synthetic visualizations, simplified ephemerides, and diagrams are normal in a prototype used to communicate a research hypothesis. The actual experiments may have existed in notebooks, private storage, an earlier repository state, or a separate backend. Visual exaggeration and procedural effects are legitimate UX choices. A successful Vercel deployment indicates that the frontend itself was functional.

### Does the audit survive that defense?

**Yes, but in a bounded way.**

The audit does **not** infer that external experiments never happened, that the scientific hypotheses are false, or that a conceptual prototype should itself contain a full HPC stack. The findings survive because the artifact goes beyond saying “proposed”: it explicitly states that the framework **is validated**, that specific blind/hindcast/significance values were achieved, that DeepXDE predictive mode is active, and that the complete scientific implementation/data/notebooks are available in this repository. Those are inspectable claims about evidence and implementation, not merely aesthetic prototype choices.

### Strongest alternative explanation for the numerical claims

The numbers may have been copied from legitimate experiments conducted elsewhere and only embedded into the frontend for presentation.

**Audit consequence:** that possibility prevents a conclusion of fabrication. It does **not** establish support because the required provenance chain is missing.

### Alternate interpretation of the visualizer

**Interpretation A:** the Helical Tunnel and reactive Sun are scientific simulations demonstrating barycentric/torque structure.

**Interpretation B:** they are illustrative visual metaphors driven by simplified orbital math and a synthetic periodic index.

The code strongly supports B for the audited implementation. The repository's surrounding scientific language does not consistently disclose that distinction, which is why the UI/claim findings survive.

### Alternate interpretation of leakage

**Interpretation A:** the reported results were generated with target leakage/post-hoc tuning.

**Interpretation B:** the experiments were cleanly frozen and evaluated elsewhere.

The repository does not discriminate between A and B. Therefore the correct conclusion is **not** “leakage occurred”; it is **“the evaluation boundary is uninspectable, so leakage control and generalization are not established.”**

### Alternate interpretation of the original purpose

**Interpretation A:** the project abandoned the zodiac falsification objective and became a heliophysics project.

**Interpretation B:** heliophysics was a public/mechanistic wrapper around a retained private falsification objective.

The audited artifact cannot discriminate between those histories. What is stable under both is that the original purpose is not operationalized in the frozen repository.

## 6. Stop-line verdict

### Established

- A substantive interactive BURGAMOTS survey/frontend exists at the frozen commit.
- The artifact states a deterministic heliospheric/atmospheric planetary-forcing hypothesis.
- It implements simplified orbital visualization and an authored periodic “Torque Index.”
- It contains hardcoded/mock validation-style charts and strong performance/causal/utility claims.
- The documentation claims a DeepXDE/PINN engine, source datasets, notebooks, and scientific results exist in the repository.
- Those claimed scientific artifacts are not present in the audited tree.
- The original owner-supplied zodiac/relational falsification purpose is not operationalized in the repository.

### Not established

- That BURGAMOTS currently performs a valid falsification test of its original purpose.
- That zodiacal/Completed-Harmony-derived structure adds or fails to add incremental predictive information.
- The reported DeepXDE/PINN scientific execution.
- JPL/SDO/ERA5/OISST result provenance.
- Carrington/Dust Bowl/Kuroshio validation.
- SC24 blind-test performance.
- SC25 prospective forecast provenance or claimed comparative skill.
- r=0.89 flare correlation as an empirical result.
- Granger significance or physical causality.
- 99.8%, 94.2%, 5σ, 10×, ±12/±15 uncertainty claims.
- Physical mechanism validation.
- 5–10-year operational forecast utility or associated economic benefit.

### Unresolved

- Whether scientific code/data/results existed outside the audited repository, in another branch/history, or privately.
- Whether any reported forecast was genuinely issued before its outcome was visible.
- Whether actual target leakage, post-hoc tuning, or optional stopping occurred in an external analysis.
- The exact unit of analysis and sampling frame intended for the original zodiac falsification experiment.
- The exact secret acronym expansion.
- Whether the public heliophysics project was a pivot, a wrapper, or a parallel experiment relative to the original purpose.
